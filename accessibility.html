Accessibility

TerraSense Jukebox is designed to be used by sound, touch, keyboard or screen reader, with no sight needed.

Keyboard

The full list of shortcuts will appear here when the player is finished. On the decoder test page, focus the frame and use the arrow keys to move; hold Shift to move faster.

Headphones and volume

Headphones give the best result, because left and right on the map will be heard as left and right. Start with your volume low.

What has been tested

Nothing has been tested with users yet. This section will say exactly what was tested, and with whom, once testing has happened.
