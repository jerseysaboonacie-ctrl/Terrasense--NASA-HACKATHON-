Hear the Earth.

Explore NASA Earth Information Center visualizations by sound, touch or keyboard, with no sight needed.

Open the player

Start audio plays a short sound check (low, middle and high notes) so you can set your volume. Start with your volume low.

  • Real NASA frames. Visualizations from NASA's Earth Information Center, served from this site.
  • Sound made live. The tone is generated in your browser from the colour under your finger or cursor.
  • Built for every way in. Designed for keyboard, touch or screen reader.

Try this: close your eyes and find the highest region on the map.

Values are approximate. They are decoded from the colors of the visualization, not from the underlying measurements.

TerraSense Jukebox is a hackathon project. It is not endorsed by NASA. Visualizations are credited to their original sources on the Credits page.
