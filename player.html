    Keyboard help

    KeyAction
    Arrow keysMove the pointer on the map
    Shift + arrowMove faster
    Enter or SpacePlay the current point again
    WWhere am I: the value and place in full, and the nearest country
    Tap the map 5 times quicklyWhere am I, on a touch screen (same as W)
    TStart the tour, or go to its next step
    DStart or stop the one-minute demo
    VTerra on or off. While on, Terra listens and answers out loud whenever you speak; say "Terra, are you still listening?" to check.
    Alt + Shift + V, Alt + Shift + WTerra on or off, Where am I, from anywhere, even when a screen reader uses V and W itself (Chrome and Edge on Windows; access keys differ by browser)
    SStart or stop the scan
    MMute or unmute
    Minus, plusVolume down, volume up
    A, BMark the current point as A or B
    CCompare A and B (plays both, then says the difference)
    EscapeStop the sound, the speech, the tour and the demo
    ?Open this help
    TabLeave the map and go to the controls

    Letter keys work anywhere on this page except in a menu or text box. With a screen reader they reach the page when the map has focus (the map passes keys through) or in focus (forms) mode; in browse mode your screen reader may use them for its own navigation. The buttons and access keys always work.
