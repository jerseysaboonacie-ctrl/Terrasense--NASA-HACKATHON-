Page tests

Loads every page of the site and checks its structure for screen-reader and keyboard use: language, title, one main heading, no skipped heading levels, landmarks, a working skip link, image text, named controls, unique ids, and no script errors. Then checks what each page is for (gallery cards, credits, Home sound check). These are automated checks of our code; they do not replace a person using a real screen reader.
